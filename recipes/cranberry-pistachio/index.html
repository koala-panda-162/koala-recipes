Cranberry–Pistachio Frangipane Galette

Your free-form pistachio frangipane galette, using supermarket pie crust, tart-sweet cranberries, egg-washed edges and roasted chopped pistachios. Updated with the adjustments from your first bake.

1 galette · 6–8 slices375°F / 190°C35–45 min estimated bake15 min assembled chillStore-bought pie crust

Crust & baking supplies

🧁 No scale? No problem. All ingredients below include approximate US measuring cup/spoon equivalents. Use level measuring spoons and cups, not heaping ones. Measure the whole shelled pistachios before grinding and the roasted nuts before chopping. Volume conversions are especially approximate for berries and nuts. Based on King Arthur Baking’s ingredient weight chart.

Pistachio frangipane (about 243 g)

Cranberries

Cranberry sweetness options: The original formulation uses 25 g sugar for 120 g cranberries (quite tart). For a more balanced sweet-tart result, try 35 g (≈ 2 tbsp + 2½ tsp); for a sweeter version, use 45 g (≈ 3 tbsp + 2 tsp). I suggested increasing to 45 g after you tasted the raw cranberries—it was an optional flavor adjustment, not a necessary correction.

Egg wash & final garnish

Shopping totals: 2 large eggs; 83 g sugar for the original version (≈ ¼ cup for frangipane + 2 tbsp for cranberries + 2 tsp for crust). For sweeter cranberry options, total sugar is 93 g (35 g cranberry sugar) or 103 g (45 g cranberry sugar). Pistachios: 75 g total (≈ ½ cup shelled raw for filling + 2 tbsp shelled for roasting and garnishing). Buy raw unsalted pistachios and roast only the garnish portion.

Method

  1. Prepare the pistachios: Pulse 60 g (≈ ½ cup) raw shelled pistachios, measured before grinding until finely ground like coarse almond flour. Avoid blending into pistachio butter. Set aside 15 g (≈ 2 tbsp) roasted pistachios for the garnish; if starting with raw, roast that portion at 325°F (165°C) for 5–8 minutes, cool and chop.
  2. Prepare the crust: Place a refrigerated rolled pie crust on parchment on a baking sheet. Unroll and, if possible, gently roll to a roughly 12-inch (30 cm) circle. Keep cold.
  3. Make the frangipane: Cream 50 g (3½ tbsp) softened butter with 50 g (¼ cup) sugar. Beat in 1 large egg and ¾ tsp vanilla. Stir in the ground pistachios from ½ cup raw shelled nuts (≈ 60 g), 20 g (3 tbsp + 1 tsp) almond flour and 10 g (1 tbsp + 1 tsp) all-purpose flour until thick and spreadable.
  4. Prepare cranberries: Toss 120 g (≈ 1¼ cups) fresh or frozen cranberries with 25 g (2 tbsp) sugar for the original tart version; use 35 g (2 tbsp + 2½ tsp) for a sweeter balance or 45 g (3 tbsp + 2 tsp) for the sweeter variation. No pre-cooking needed; do not thaw frozen cranberries.
  5. Fill & fold: Spread the pistachio frangipane over the center of the crust, keeping a 2-inch (5 cm) border. Distribute sugared cranberries on top. Fold and pleat the crust inward, pinching gaps shut.
  6. Chill: Refrigerate the assembled galette for 15 minutes. Preheat a conventional oven to 375°F (190°C).
  7. Egg wash: Beat the second egg with 1 tsp water. Lightly brush only the exposed crust and sprinkle with 8 g (≈ 2 tsp) sugar.
  8. Bake: Bake on the middle rack at 375°F (190°C), approximately 35–45 minutes. Since egg wash browns more quickly, check the crust at 25–30 minutes and then every 5 minutes. The frangipane should be puffed and set (not wet or liquid), cranberries softened and some burst, and the underside of the crust golden. If the crust becomes dark before the center sets, shield edges loosely with foil and continue.
  9. Cool & finish: Cool for at least 45 minutes, then sprinkle the roasted, roughly chopped 15 g (≈ 2 tbsp before chopping) pistachios on top.

Store-bought crust sizing: These full filling quantities were developed for a 12-inch pastry circle. If your ready-made sheet remains closer to 11 inches, use approximately 75–80% of the prepared frangipane and cranberries to prevent overflow. Bake any excess egg-containing frangipane separately until set.
